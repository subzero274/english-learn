## 七、实用例句

1. **We can work on our report on the research we did together.**
   > 我们可以一起写我们共同研究的报告。

2. **We need something about what causes different organisms to choose particular habitats.**
   > 我们需要写一些关于什么导致不同生物选择特定栖息地的内容。

3. **I was surprised how straightforward that was.**
   > 我很惊讶那竟然这么简单。

4. **We need to discuss the effects of wave action.**
   > 我们需要讨论海浪冲击的影响。

5. **Our general method of observation seemed quite reliable.**
   > 我们总的观察方法似乎相当可靠。

6. **The descriptions weren't very detailed, but they were adequate in this context.**
   > 这些描述不是很详细，但在这个背景下已经足够了。
