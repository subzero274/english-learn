## 二、错误类型统计

| 错误类型 | 出现次数 | 举例 |
|---|---|---|
| **拼写错误** | 约 25 处 | persional, assessnment, exexceise, avalivale, preimer |
| **单复数错误** | 约 6 处 | club→clubs, type→types, weight→weights, cloth→clothes |
| **动词形式错误** | 约 5 处 | start→starts, cost→costs, give→gives |
| **冠词缺失/错误** | 约 4 处 | use club→use the club, a club→the club |
| **介词错误** | 约 3 处 | join→During, on→to, for→when |
| **连读/弱读听错** | 多处 | making→major, poud→pounds, indi wg→Individual |
| **完全听错** | 约 4 处 | do in revilvo→avoid having, that looking more→there's a lot more |

## 三、主要改进建议

1. **加强基础词汇拼写**：personal, assessment, exercise, available, equipment 等高频词要记牢。
2. **注意第三人称单数**：主语是 this/membership/LP 等单数时，动词要加 -s。
3. **注意单复数**：membership 不可数，clothes 通常复数，equipment 不可数。
4. **熟悉连读弱读**：如 Individual /ˌɪndɪˈvɪdʒuəl/，available /əˈveɪləbl/，pounds /paʊndz/。
5. **注意固定搭配**：
   - find **it** easier to do sth
   - **There's** a lot more to... than...
   - **have to have** sth
   - **be able to** do sth

## 四、原文完整对照

> This starts on day one with your personal assessment. You are asked to fill in a questionnaire giving details of any health problems. One of our personal trainers will then go through this with you. The trainer will then take you through the safety rules for using the equipment in the fitness suite. During your next exercise session a personal trainer will work with you to make sure you understand these. It's very important to do this because we really do want to avoid having any sports injuries. There's a lot more to looking after yourself than simply lifting weights!

> Now, I'll just quickly run through the types of membership we have available. All members must pay a joining fee of 90 in addition to the rates for the monthly membership fees. Gold membership entitles you to free entry at all LP Clubs. There are now LP clubs in all major cities and towns so if you travel a lot this will be a great advantage. Individual gold membership costs 50 a month and joint membership for you and your partner will cost 75. Premier membership is for professional people whose work commitments make it difficult for them to use the club during the day and so LP gives booking preferences to Premier members at peak times. This means you will find it easier to book the sessions at times that suit you. Reciprocal arrangements with other LP Clubs are available to Premier members.

> You don't have to have any special clothes or equipment when you visit the club. We provide robes and hairdryers in the changing rooms, but it's very important to remember your photo card because you won't be able to get in without it.
