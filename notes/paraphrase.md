## 听力同义替换（IELTS Listening）

### 10-1-1 同义替换（World Tours）

| 题目表达 | 原文表达 | 说明 |
|----------|----------|------|
| heard about ... from | read about ... in the newspaper | 信息来源：from → in |
| some ___ parks | theme parks | 题目省略了修饰词 theme |
| not a ___ | staying in a tent / campsite | campsite = tent（地点→住宿方式） |
| on the way to Cambria | near Cambria | 方位同义替换 |
| wants to see | I'd like to do that | 意愿表达 |
| spend time on the beach | sunbathing and swimming | 地点 → 活动 |
| total distance | covers ... kilometres | covers 表示"覆盖/总里程" |
| one ___ | a flight | one = a |
| included in the price | offer dinner in the price | 包含关系 |
| cheaper | almost a hundred pounds cheaper | 价格比较 |
| not included | They aren't included | 否定信息明确化 |

### 11-3-1 同义替换与数字陷阱（Car Hire / 租车咨询）

| 题目表达 | 原文表达 | 说明 |
|----------|----------|------|
| put four down | — | 题目写 3 但实际是 **4**，数字题别急着写 |

## 常见雅思听力同义替换分类

### 1. 信息来源 / 渠道

| 题目常见词 | 原文可能替换 | 例句 |
|------------|--------------|------|
| heard about | read about, saw, found out about | "I read about you in the newspaper." |
| from a friend | someone told me, a friend recommended | "Someone told me there's a nice castle." |
| advert / advertisement | saw it somewhere, in the paper, on TV | "Did you see an advert somewhere?" |
| online | website, on the Internet, by email | "I found it on your website." |

### 2. 方位 / 位置

| 题目常见词 | 原文可能替换 | 例句 |
|------------|--------------|------|
| on the way to | near, close to, on that road, on the route to | "Hearst Castle is on that road." |
| in the center | in the middle, downtown, in the city centre | "It's in the city centre." |
| next to | beside, adjacent to, near, by | "The hotel is next to the station." |
| opposite | across from, facing | "The bank is opposite the post office." |
| in the north | to the north, northern part | "It's in the northern part of the city." |
| near the entrance | by the door, at the front | "Please wait near the entrance." |

### 3. 价格 / 费用

| 题目常见词 | 原文可能替换 | 例句 |
|------------|--------------|------|
| cost / price | charge, fee, rate, pay | "The cost is £525 per person." |
| per person | each, for one person, a head | "It's £429 per person." |
| included | covered, in the price, free of charge | "That includes accommodation." |
| not included | extra, not covered, you have to pay for | "Flights aren't included." |
| cheaper | lower price, less expensive, a hundred pounds cheaper | "It's almost a hundred pounds cheaper." |
| expensive | dear, costly, high price | "It's quite expensive." |
| free | no charge, complimentary, at no cost | "Breakfast is free of charge." |

### 4. 距离 / 时间 / 数字

| 题目常见词 | 原文可能替换 | 例句 |
|------------|--------------|------|
| total distance | covers, the journey is, it takes ... km | "covers 2,020 kilometres" |
| duration | lasts, takes, for ... days | "The tour lasts twelve days." |
| about / approximately | around, roughly, just over, nearly | "It covers about 980 km." |
| 12 days | two-week trip, a fortnight | "It's a twelve-day trip." |
| 2,020 | two thousand and twenty | 数字写法和读法转换 |
| 50% | half, fifty percent | "Half of the students are local." |

### 5. 动作 / 活动 / 意愿

| 题目常见词 | 原文可能替换 | 例句 |
|------------|--------------|------|
| want to | would like to, interested in, keen on, plan to | "I'd like to do that." |
| visit | go to, see, stop at, have a look at | "We want to visit some theme parks." |
| spend time on | relax at, do sunbathing and swimming | "spend time on the beach" |
| include | contain, involve, cover | "The price includes dinner." |
| do some shopping | go shopping, buy things | "stop and do some shopping" |
| recommend | suggest, advise | "I suggest you book early." |
| book | reserve, make a reservation | "I'd like to book a room." |

### 6. 住宿 / 餐饮

| 题目常见词 | 原文可能替换 | 例句 |
|------------|--------------|------|
| stay | spend the night, accommodation | "You can stay in a lodge." |
| hotel | lodge, inn, guest house, hostel | "You can choose a lodge." |
| campsite | tent, camping | "staying in a tent" |
| meals | breakfast, lunch, dinner, food | "no meals" / "dinner is included" |
| breakfast included | bed and breakfast, B&B | "Breakfast is included in the price." |
| self-catering | cook for yourself, no meals provided | "It's self-catering accommodation." |

### 7. 交通 / 旅行

| 题目常见词 | 原文可能替换 | 例句 |
|------------|--------------|------|
| hire a car | rent a car, car rental | "We want to hire a car." |
| self-drive | drive yourself, by car | "self-drive tours" |
| flight | plane, air travel | "a flight is included" |
| return ticket | round trip | "a return ticket costs ..." |
| direct | non-stop | "It's a direct flight." |
| timetable | schedule | "Check the bus timetable." |
| depart | leave, set off | "The train departs at 9." |
| arrive | get to, reach | "We arrive at noon." |

### 8. 频率 / 程度

| 题目常见词 | 原文可能替换 | 例句 |
|------------|--------------|------|
| always | every time, constantly | "I always take the bus." |
| usually | normally, generally, most of the time | "I usually walk." |
| sometimes | occasionally, now and then | "Sometimes I cycle." |
| never | not at all, rarely | "I never drive." |
| very | extremely, really, quite | "It's very expensive." |
| a little | a bit, slightly, not much | "It's a bit cold." |
| a lot of | plenty of, lots of, a large number of | "plenty of time" |

### 9. 否定 / 转折（信号词）

| 题目/选项 | 原文信号 | 说明 |
|-----------|----------|------|
| not | no, isn't, aren't, don't, doesn't | "No, I read about you in the newspaper." |
| but | however, actually, in fact, although | "But these hotels offer dinner." |
| rather than | instead of, not ... but | "I want a lodge, not a tent." |
| I don't think so | not really, probably not | "I don't think so. We want to go swimming." |

### 10. 人名 / 地址 / 拼写

| 题目常见词 | 原文表达 | 注意事项 |
|------------|----------|----------|
| name | first name, surname, family name | 区分名和姓 |
| address | street, road, avenue, lane | "24 Ardleigh Road" |
| postcode | ZIP code | 英国用 postcode |
| mobile | cell phone | "Is a mobile alright?" |
| spell it | can you spell that | 注意每个字母 |

## 补充替换 / Extra Paraphrases

| 原表达 | 常见听力替换 | 例句 / 说明 |
|--------|--------------|-------------|
| address | location, where you live, place of residence, where ... is situated | "Could I have your address?" = "Where do you live?" |
| behind | at the back of, round the back, rear of, the other side of | "The kitchen is behind the counter." |
| opposite | across from, facing, directly across, on the other side of the road | "The bank is opposite the post office." |
| next to | beside, adjacent to, by, alongside, neighboring | "The cafe is next to the library." |
| above | over, on the upper floor, upstairs, higher than, on the ... floor | "The office is above the shop." |
| approximately | about, around, roughly, just over, just under, nearly, almost | "It costs approximately £50." = "about £50" |
| in advance | beforehand, ahead of time, before you go, book ahead, early | "You need to book in advance." |
| show | demonstrate, display, exhibition, performance, present, guide around, take around | "The museum shows local art." = "displays local art" |
| and | as well as, also, in addition to, plus, together with, along with | "tea and coffee" = "tea as well as coffee" |
| currently | at the moment, at present, now, right now, these days | "I'm currently working in Beijing." |
| during the week | on weekdays, Monday to Friday, not at the weekend, from Monday through Friday | "We're open during the week." |
| does a lot of cycling | cycles regularly, rides a bike a lot, goes cycling frequently, keen on cycling | "He does a lot of cycling." = "He rides a bike frequently." |
| be interested in | would like to, want to, keen on, fond of, attracted to, thinking about | "I'm interested in going to California." = "I'd like to go to California." |
