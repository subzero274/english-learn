## 逐题解析

### 1. Address: 24 ___ Road

**原文：**
> "And your address?"  
> "24 Ardleigh Road."  
> "Can you spell that?"  
> "A-R-D-L-E-I-G-H Road."

**定位词：** address, 24, Road, spell
**答案：** Ardleigh
**注意：** 拼写题是 Section 1 经典考点，注意字母顺序和易错点（Ardleigh 不是 Ardleight）。

---

### 2. Heard about company from: ___

**原文：**
> "And can I ask you where you heard about World Tours? From a friend? Or did you see an advert somewhere?"
> "No, I read about you in the newspaper."

**定位词：** heard about World Tours, from
**答案：** newspaper
**同义替换：**
- heard about → read about
- from → in（信息来源的介词转换）

**注意：** 这里用了一个否定回答（No），否定了 "friend" 和 "advert"，然后给出真正答案 newspaper。这种"先否定干扰项，再给出答案"是听力常见套路。

---

### 3. Los Angeles: customer wants to visit some ___ parks with her children

**原文：**
> "The first one begins in Los Angeles and there's plenty of time to visit some of the theme parks there."
> "That's something on my children's list so I'd want to include that."

**定位词：** Los Angeles, children, parks, want to visit
**答案：** theme
**同义替换：**
- 题目：some ___ parks → 原文：some of the **theme parks**
- parks 是原文的省略形式，需要识别出 theme parks 是固定搭配
- want to visit → want to include（都表示想要安排）

**注意：** 不要只写 parks，因为题目已经给出 parks，需要填的是修饰词 theme。

---

### 4. Yosemite Park: customer wants to stay in a lodge, not a ___

**原文：**
> "You can choose to stay in a lodge or on the campsite."
> "I don't like the idea of staying in a tent. It'd be too hot."

**定位词：** Yosemite Park, lodge, not, campsite
**答案：** tent
**同义替换：**
- campsite = tent（露营地里住帐篷）
- 不是 lodge 的否定，而是"不愿住帐篷"

**注意：** 原文先说 campsite，后解释 tent，考生需要把 campsite 和 tent 关联起来。题目中的 "not a ___" 结构是对原文 "I don't like... staying in a tent" 的直接对应。

---

### 5. Customer wants to see the ___ on the way to Cambria

**原文：**
> "Then you drive south to Cambria."
> "Someone told me there's a really nice castle near Cambria. Will we go near that?"
> "Hearst Castle is on that road so you could stop there."
> "Good. I'd like to do that."

**定位词：** Cambria, castle, see, on the way
**答案：** castle / Hearst Castle
**同义替换：**
- near Cambria → on the way to Cambria
- stop there → see
- I'd like to do that = wants to see

**注意：** 答案可以写 castle（简洁），也可以写 Hearst Castle（完整）。填空题通常只要求关键词。

---

### 6. At San Diego, wants to spend time on the ___

**原文：**
> "you could go straight on to San Diego."
> "That's good for beaches isn't it?"
> "that's a good place to relax and your children might like to visit the zoo before flying home."
> "I don't think so. We want some time for sunbathing and swimming."

**定位词：** San Diego, spend time on the
**答案：** beach / beaches
**同义替换：**
- sunbathing and swimming = beach（在海滩上进行的活动）
- 题目用 "the beach" 单数，原文用 "beaches" 复数，都可以接受

**注意：** 这里是反向排除法：否定了 zoo，肯定了 beach activities（sunbathing and swimming）。

---

## 听力技巧总结

1. **Section 1 拼写题**：遇到名字、地址、邮编时，注意字母拼写和数字的准确记录。
2. **否定转折**："No, I don't think so" 后面往往是真正答案。
3. **同义替换**：park → theme park, campsite → tent, sunbathing and swimming → beach。
4. **先听后填**：不要听到什么词就填什么，要确认题目结构（如题目已给 parks，只缺 theme）。

### 7. Total distance: ___ km

**原文：**
> "The first one I told you about is a self-drive tour through California which lasts twelve days and covers 2,020 kilometres."

**定位词：** Total distance, 12 days, Trip One, covers
**答案：** 2020 / 2,020
**同义替换：**
- covers = total distance（覆盖的距离 = 总距离）
- kilometres = km

**注意：** 数字题要注意千位分隔符（comma）。IELTS 填空接受 "2020" 或 "2,020"。听到 "two thousand and twenty" 时，建议先写数字，再确认格式。

---

### 8. Includes: one ___

**原文：**
> "That includes accommodation, car rental and a flight but no meals."

**定位词：** Includes, accommodation, car, one
**答案：** flight
**同义替换：**
- a flight = one flight（a 和 one 对应）
- includes = 直接重现

**注意：** 这里是并列结构：accommodation, car rental, a flight。题目已经给出 accommodation 和 car，要填的是第三个并列项。不要受后面 "but no meals" 干扰。

---

### 9. Price (per person): £ ___

**原文：**
> "So is that cheaper then?"
> "Yes, it's almost a hundred pounds cheaper. It's 429 per person, which is a good deal."

**定位词：** Price, per person, Trip Two, cheaper
**答案：** 429
**同义替换：**
- "almost a hundred pounds cheaper" 是价格提示，暗示比 525 低约 100
- per person = per person（直接重现）

**注意：** 前面的 Trip One 价格是 525，这里容易误填成差值。要听到 "It's 429 per person" 直接记录数字。

---

### 10. Includes: ___

**原文：**
> "So that covers accommodation and car hire. What about flights?"
> "They aren't included. But these hotels offer dinner in the price."

**定位词：** Includes, Trip Two, accommodation, car, flights, dinner
**答案：** dinner
**同义替换：**
- offer dinner in the price = includes dinner（包含在价格中 = 包含）
- They aren't included（flights 不包含）— 这是排除干扰项

**注意：** 这里先否定了 flights，再给出 dinner。不要被 "flights" 干扰，题目问的是 Trip Two 包含什么，而 flights 明确说了 "aren't included"。

## 你的错误分析

| 题号 | 你的答案 | 正确答案 | 问题分析 |
|------|----------|----------|----------|
| 1 | ardleight | **Ardleigh** | 多了一个字母 t。原文拼写是 A-R-D-L-E-I-G-H，注意最后三个字母是 I-G-H，不是 I-G-H-T。 |
| 2 | newspaper | **newspaper** | ✅ 正确 |
| 3 | things | **theme** | 你可能听到了 "something on my children's list" 里的 things，但题目问的是 parks 的类型，答案是 theme parks。注意区分题目结构和同义替换。 |
| 4 | campu | **tent** | 听到了 campsite 但没写完整，且真正答案是她不想住的 **tent**。 campsite 是地点，tent 是具体住宿方式。 |
| 5 | cansel | **castle** | 拼写错误，且可能没听清。原文是 castle（城堡），不是 cancel。注意开头音 /kæ/ 相同，但结尾完全不同。 |
| 6 | swimming | **beach** | swimming 是 beach 上的活动，但题目要求 "ONE WORD" 且问的是地点。 sunbathing and swimming 共同指向 beach。 |

### 改进建议

1. **拼写题**：边听边写，听完后立刻核对字母顺序，尤其是地名（Ardleigh, castle）。
2. **区分干扰项**：听到 "No" / "I don't think so" 后，真正的答案才出来。
3. **题目结构优先**：先看题目缺什么词性/含义，再填。不要听到熟悉的词就写。
4. **同义替换积累**：theme parks, campsite=tent, sunbathing and swimming=beach，这些都是常考替换。
