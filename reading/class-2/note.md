## 三、题型笔记：有选项填空题

### 特点

- 多为**顺序题**（答案在文中按顺序出现）。
- 存在**复选**情况：有且只有一个选项会被选 **2 次**。
- 答案多为**同义替换**，不是原词重现。

### 解题策略

1. **先读题干**，划出关键词。
2. **按顺序回原文定位**，不要跳读。
3. 注意 **一个选项用两次** 的情况，不要因为选过一次就排除。
4. 答案通常是原文的 **同义表达**，而不是原词。
5. 注意 **词性匹配**：填空处需要什么词性，选项就要对应什么词性。

## 四、例句参考

1. **livestock**
   > The farm raises **livestock** such as cows, sheep and pigs.

2. **proof**
   > There is no **proof** that this method works.

3. **plantation**
   > The tea **plantation** covers hundreds of acres.

4. **harbour**
   > The city has a natural **harbour** that protects ships from storms.

5. **archaeological discovery**
   > The **archaeological discovery** changed our understanding of ancient civilizations.

6. **bury**
   > The ancient city was **buried** under sand for thousands of years.

7. **nature vs nurture**
   > The debate about **nature versus nurture** has lasted for centuries.

8. **lead**
   > Old pipes may contain **lead**, which is harmful to health.

9. **subject**
   > All **subjects** in the experiment were between 20 and 30 years old.
