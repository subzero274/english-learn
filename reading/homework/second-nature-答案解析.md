## 二、逐题解析

### 14. transformation ✅

**题目**：Psychologists have traditionally believed that a personality ____ was impossible.

**原文**：Psychologists have long held that a person's character cannot undergo a **transformation** in any meaningful way.

**解析**：直接原词重现。

---

### 15. young age ✅

**题目**：...and that by a ____ a person's character tends to be fixed.

**原文**：...the key traits of personality are determined at a very **young age**.

**解析**：直接原词重现。

---

### 16. optimism ✅

**题目**：One of the easiest qualities to acquire is ____.

**原文**：Some qualities are less challenging to develop than others, **optimism** being one of them.

**解析**：直接定位。

---

### 17. skills ✅

**题目**：...it is necessary to learn a wide variety of different ____ in order for a new quality to develop.

**原文**：...developing qualities requires mastering a range of **skills** which are diverse and sometimes surprising.

**解析**：a range of skills = a wide variety of different skills。

---

### 18. cultivating ❌

**你的答案**：cultivating  
**正确答案**：negative emotions

**题目**：...a person must understand and feel some ____ in order to increase their happiness.

**原文**：to bring more joy and passion into your life, you must be open to experiencing **negative emotions**.

**错因**：
1. 语法上，"feel some ____" 需要填名词，cultivating 是动名词，但语义不通。
2. 原文明确说想获得更多快乐，必须先愿意体验 **negative emotions**。

---

### 19. E (Todd Kashdan) ✅

**题目**：People must accept that they do not know much when first trying something new.

**原文**：Psychologist Todd Kashdan has this advice for those people taking up a new passion: "As a newcomer, you also have to tolerate and laugh at your own **ignorance**."

**解析**："do not know much" 对应 "ignorance"，人物是 Todd Kashdan。

---

### 20. C (Suzanne Segerstrom) ✅

**题目**：It is important for people to actively notice when good things happen.

**原文**：Suzanne Segerstrom... recommends you train yourself to **pay attention to good fortune** by writing down three positive things that come about each day.

**解析**："actively notice when good things happen" 对应 "pay attention to good fortune"。

---

### 21. G (Cynthia Pury) ❌

**你的答案**：F (Kenneth Pedeleose)  
**正确答案**：G (Cynthia Pury)

**题目**：Courage can be learned once its origins in a sense of responsibility are understood.

**原文**：According to **Cynthia Pury**... courage is not motivated by fearlessness, but by **moral obligation**. Pury also believes that people can acquire courage.

**错因**：
- Kenneth Pedeleose 是**勇敢行为的例子**（实际去揭发欺凌的人）。
- Cynthia Pury 是**提出观点的人**：勇气源于道德责任，而非无所畏惧，且可以被学会。
- 题目问的是 "courage can be learned once its origins... are understood" 这个观点，对应 Pury。

---

### 22. A (Christopher Peterson) ❌

**你的答案**：G (Cynthia Pury)  
**正确答案**：A (Christopher Peterson)

**题目**：It is possible to overcome shyness when faced with the need to speak in public.

**原文**：**Christopher Peterson**... Inherently introverted, he realised early on that as an academic, his **reticence** would prove disastrous in the lecture hall. So he learned to be more outgoing and to entertain his classes.

**错因**：
- "overcome shyness" 对应 "learned to be more outgoing"。
- "speak in public" 对应 "in the lecture hall"。
- 这是 Christopher Peterson 自己的例子，不是 Cynthia Pury。

### 23. E ✅

**题目**：a mention of how rational thinking enabled someone to achieve physical goals

**正确答案**：E

**原文**：Section E — Tanya Streeter: "Streeter learned to **untangle her fears from her judgment** of what her body and mind could do."

**解析**："rational thinking" 对应 "judgment"，"achieve physical goals" 对应 "what her body and mind could do"（自由潜水创造世界纪录）。

## 三、主要问题总结

1. **18 题**：没有注意 "feel some ____" 后面需要接情感类名词，误填了 cultivating。
2. **21-22 题**：人物观点和人物例子混淆。Cynthia Pury 是观点提出者，Christopher Peterson 是自我改变的例子。
3. **24-26 题**：C、G、H 三个段落的主人公和事件混淆。

## 五、改进建议

1. **填空题**：注意空前后的词性要求，尤其是 "feel / experience / some / a" 等词后面通常接名词。
2. **人物匹配题**：区分 "谁提出了观点" 和 "谁是例子"。
3. **段落匹配题**：先快速浏览每段首句，标记人物和事件，再做题。
4. **复盘方法**：把错题对应的原文句子抄下来，标注同义替换。
