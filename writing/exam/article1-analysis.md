## 二、改写范文

The table compares the average distance travelled per person per year in England across eight different modes of transport in 1985 and 2000.

Overall, the total distance travelled rose considerably, from 4,740 miles in 1985 to 6,475 miles in 2000. Car travel was by far the most popular mode throughout the period, while walking and bicycle use remained the least common.

In terms of increases, car travel saw the largest rise, climbing from 3,199 miles to 4,806 miles. Long-distance bus use more than doubled, growing from 54 miles to 124 miles, and train travel also increased, from 289 to 366 miles. Smaller rises could be seen in taxi and other categories, with taxi use going up from 13 to 42 miles.

By contrast, walking, cycling and local bus use all declined. Walking fell slightly from 255 to 237 miles, while bicycle use dropped from 51 to 41 miles. The most dramatic decrease, however, was in local bus travel, which fell sharply from 429 to 274 miles.

### 练习 3：同义替换

1. Car travel **had a bigger up**, rising by 1,605 miles.

2. Long-distance bus use **had a fast increase**.  
   → Long-distance bus use **__________** rapidly.
3. Local bus **was decrease** dramatically.  
   → Local bus travel **__________** dramatically.
4. Walking **was down** from 255 to 237 miles.  
   → Walking **__________** from 255 to 237 miles.

2. increased / rose / grew  
3. fell / dropped / declined  
4. fell / dropped / decreased
