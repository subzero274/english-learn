题目

In modern society, it is possible to shop, work, and communicate via the internet without face-to-face contact with one another. Do you think it is a positive or a negative development?
Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience. Write at least 250 words.

练习 1：拼写纠错

补全句子，写出括号提示单词的正确形式。

1. analyze, sides   2. replace, disadvantages   3. first   4. buy, world   5. possible, spend   6. companies   7. knowledge, science

练习 2：语法改错

请改写以下句子，修正语法错误。

1. In modern society, people can shop, work and communicate via the Internet.
2. I will analyze both the positive and negative sides.
3. Using the Internet to replace face-to-face contact has many disadvantages.
4. It may make people feel lonely or irritable.
5. People can buy products from all over the world at the cheapest price.
6. It makes it possible to work from home.
7. I suggest that everyone use the Internet to do almost anything.

练习 3：同义替换

选择更正式或更多样的表达。

1. beneficial development   2. health problems   3. purchase   4. commuting

练习 4：观点扩展

选择一个论点，用「观点 + 解释 + 例子 + 结果」结构扩展成 3-4 句话。

建议包含：work from home, commute, spend, example, as a result
Working online is more efficient because employees no longer need to spend hours commuting every day. For example, many technology companies allow their staff to work from home for one or two days a week. As a result, workers can use the saved time for rest, exercise or family, which may improve both their productivity and quality of life.
