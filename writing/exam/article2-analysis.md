# IELTS Task 2 大作文批改与训练 · article2

> 题目：In modern society, it is possible to shop, work, and communicate via the internet without face-to-face contact with one another. Do you think it is a positive or a negative development?

## 二、改写范文

In modern society, people can shop, work and communicate online without meeting each other in person. In my opinion, this is largely a positive development, although it does have some negative effects.

On the one hand, relying too much on the Internet can cause problems. Firstly, online communication is often limited to text, which can feel cold and impersonal. As a result, people may feel lonely or become more irritable because they cannot read body language or tone of voice. Secondly, spending long hours in front of screens can lead to health problems, such as lower back pain and eye strain.

On the other hand, the benefits of the Internet are considerable. First of all, online shopping is convenient because people can buy products from all over the world and compare prices easily. What is more, working online is more efficient. It makes it possible for people to work from home, which saves commuting time and allows them to work from anywhere. Many technology companies, for example, allow their staff to work remotely for one or two days a week. In addition, communicating through the Internet is often free or very cheap. People can send messages or make video calls without paying high phone bills. Finally, the Internet helps people study. With the help of AI and online courses, learners can study subjects such as science, English or history at a low cost and at their own pace.

Overall, although there are some disadvantages, I believe that the ability to shop, work and communicate online is a positive development. I would suggest that everyone learn to use the Internet wisely to make life easier and more productive.

### 练习 4：观点扩展

选择一个论点，用「观点 + 解释 + 例子 + 结果」结构扩展成 3-4 句话：

- 论点 A：Online shopping allows people to compare prices easily.
- 论点 B：Remote working saves commuting time.
- 论点 C：The Internet helps people study more flexibly.

<details>
<summary>参考答案（论点 B）</summary>

Working online is more efficient because employees no longer need to spend hours commuting every day. For example, many technology companies allow their staff to work from home for one or two days a week. As a result, workers can use the saved time for rest, exercise or family, which may improve both their productivity and quality of life.
