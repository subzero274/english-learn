练习 2：语法改错

请改写以下句子，修正语法错误。

1. An increase can be seen in car, long-distance bus and train travel.
2. Car travel saw the biggest increase, rising by 1,605 miles.
3. Other transport modes saw a decrease.
4. Bicycle use fell from 51 to 41 miles.
5. Overall, the average distance rose sharply, with all modes increasing from 4,740 miles to 6,475 miles.
