作业 2 最小语法修正版（只改语法/拼写，不添加连接词）：
The bar charts show what graduate and postgraduate students who did not go into full-time work did after leaving college in the UK, 2008. The figures were different in graduate and postgraduate students.

In detail, graduate students preferred to turn to further study and it was 29,665, which was the largest number in those charts. Part-time work and unemployment had similar data, respectively 17,735 and 16,235. Voluntary work was the smallest number in those charts and it was only 3,500.

A slightly different situation, meanwhile, can be seen in the postgraduates. In detail, the percentage of students choosing part-time work was noticeably high, reaching a similar level to those pursuing further study, respectively: part-time work was 2,535 and further study was 2,725. Voluntary work was similar to that for graduate students, which was the smallest number (345). Unemployment was 1,625.

To sum up, this graph shows that further study was the most popular choice for both graduate and postgraduate students, while voluntary work was the most unpopular.
