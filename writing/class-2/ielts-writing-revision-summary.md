## 三、仍存在的问题（冲刺 6.0 需改进）

### 1. 概述段偏弱

**修改后版本：**
> These charts indicate that the percentage of british Students able to speak languages other than English was divided unevenly in 10 years.

**问题：**
- "divided unevenly" 不是描述饼图的常用搭配
- 没有概括出主要趋势
- "british" 和 "Students" 仍不应大写

**建议改为：**
> Overall, the proportion of students who spoke only Spanish increased and remained the largest category, while the figure for those who spoke no other language saw the most significant fall.

### 2. 结尾总结段多余

雅思 Task 1 通常不需要单独的 "To sum up" 结尾段。应把总趋势放在第二段（overview），删除最后一段。

### 3. 词汇重复

以下词出现频率过高：
- proportion（约 8 次）
- increase / rise（多次）
- decrease / fall（多次）

**可替换为：**
- proportion → percentage / share / figure
- increase → rise / grow / climb
- decrease → fall / drop / decline
- remained unchanged → stayed the same / was stable

### 4. 衔接词可更丰富

目前主要使用：In detail, To be more specific, To sum up, While

**可补充：**
- Overall, By contrast, In contrast, Meanwhile, As for, Turning to, In terms of

### 5. 句式仍偏简单

多数句子为 "X increased/decreased from... to..."，建议加入：
- 分词结构：*rising from... to...*
- 定语从句：*which made it the largest category*
- 比较结构：*twice as much as...*

## 四、典型句子对比

### 第一句（开头改写）

> The pie charts show the proportions of british students at one university in ten years(2000-2010) in England who were able to speak other languages in addition to English.These charts indicate that the percentage of british Students able to speak languages other than English was divided unevenly in 10 years.

**6.0 目标版本：**
> The pie charts compare the proportions of British students at one university in England who were able to speak other languages in addition to English in 2000 and 2010. Overall, the distribution of language abilities among these students changed noticeably over the ten-year period.

**第一版：**

**修改后：**

**6.0 目标版本：**
> In detail, Spanish only, another language and two other languages all increased. Spanish only accounted for 30% in 2000 and then grew to 35% in 2010, making it the largest category. Similarly, the proportion of students speaking another language rose from 15% to 20%, and the figure for those speaking two other languages also climbed by 5 percentage points to 15%.

**第一版：**

## 六、关键教训

1. **拼写和基础语法是 5.5 的门槛**：第一版大量拼写和动词错误直接拉到 4.0，修改后清除这些错误后立刻提升到 5.5。
2. **国家/语言名必须大写**：British、Spanish、English、French、German 等都要大写。
3. **图表作文不要写结尾总结段**：总趋势放 overview 段。
4. **避免所有格硬凑**：不要写 "German only's percentage"，改为 "the proportion of German-only speakers"。
5. **数据描述要分组清晰**：上升、不变、下降分开写，不要混在一起。
