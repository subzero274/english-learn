## 二、动态图复习：C11T3 CO₂ 排放（Line Graph）

### 1. 四段式结构

| 段落 | 句数 | 内容 |
|------|------|------|
| 首段 | 2 句 | 改写题目 + 总括趋势（不同趋势） |
| 主体段 1 | 2–4 句 | 下降组：UK + Sweden |
| 主体段 2 | 2–4 句 | 上升组：Italy + Portugal |
| 尾段 | 1 句 | 比大小：谁始终最大/最小 |

### 2. 分段原则

- **四条线**：通常按趋势分组（两升 / 两降）。
- **写作顺序**：先写变化复杂的线或被超越的线，再写超越者，确保逻辑连贯。
- **本案例顺序**：先写下降组（UK, Sweden），再写上升组（Italy, Portugal）。

### 3. 首段改写 + 总括

> The line graph shows average emissions of CO₂ per person in metric tonnes in four countries during a long period of 40 years since 1967.
>
> In general, this report indicates that there were different trends in average carbon dioxide emissions.

**要点**：
- 首句必须包含 **时间、地点、对象、单位**。
- 单位在首段交代后，后文可用缩写（mt / Mt），避免重复拼写错误。
- 总括不要罗列细节（如“两根升两根降”），应抽象为 **different trends**。

### 4. 主体段：下降组（UK & Sweden）

#### UK 描写思路

- 中速下降，从最高点开始。
- 交代起点（最高点，具体数字和年份）和终点（最低点，具体数字和年份）。
- 强调其始终处于最高位。

#### Sweden 描写思路

- 先升后降的复杂趋势。
- 交代起点 → 峰值（年份+数字） → 终点。
- 强调终点远低于起点。

**课件例句**：

> Meanwhile, that in the latter rose quickly to about 10.5mt in 1977, which was the peak.
>
> It then fell significantly to the finishing point in 2007 (almost 5.2mt).
>
> This figure was much lower than its starting point in 1967, which was around 8.2mt.

**注意**：课件用了 **finishing point**，但更自然的表达是 **final point** 或 **end point**。

### 5. 主体段：上升组（Italy & Portugal）

#### Italy 描写思路

- 稳步上升。
- 提及 **超越 Sweden 的时间点**（1987 年），无需详述交点数值。

**课件例句**：

> Between then, there was a dramatic increase in that of Italy, which was from about 4.5mt to 7.8mt, surpassing that of Sweden in 1987.

#### Portugal 描写思路

- 上升趋势。
- 总量始终最低，但终点与 Sweden 重合。

### 6. 尾段：比大小

> To sum up, this graph shows that the UK always emitted the most CO₂ among these countries over these years.

**要点**：
- 若同一对象在起始和结束年份均为最大，强调其 **始终领先（always）**。
- 若最大者发生变化，使用 **was overtaken by / was surpassed by**。

### 7. 动态图高频错误纠正

| 错误表达 | 问题 | 正确表达 |
|---------|------|---------|
| in UK | 缺冠词 | in **the** UK |
| drop slowly | 时态应与 started 一致 | **dropped** slowly |
| at 1977 | 年份前用 in | **in** 1977 |
| top / bottom | 描述数据不够学术 | highest point / lowest point / peak |
| finish point | 不自然 | final point / end point |
| 2 times more than | 有歧义 | **twice as large as** / **more than twice as large as** |

## 三、静态单饼图：快餐偏好（Pie Chart）

### 1. 静态图基础规范

| 项目 | 要求 |
|------|------|
| 时态 | 无具体时间状语时，用 **一般现在时** |
| 首段第 2 句 | 必须指出 **均分 or 不均分** |
| 主体段 | 按 **大小** 分段 |
| 尾段 | 归纳 **常识性文字特征**，不要重复比大小 |

### 2. 数据分布描述

- **不均分**：unevenly distributed / the distribution is uneven
- **均分**：evenly distributed（较少见）
- **占比**：account for / make up / take up / occupy / constitute

### 3. 案例：美国青少年快餐偏好

**数据**：汉堡 33%、披萨 26%、薯条 22%、炸鸡 9%、塔可 7%、寿司 3%

#### 首段改写 + 总括

> The pie chart illustrates the uneven distribution of fast food preferences among teenagers in the USA.

#### 主体段分段

- **高占比组**：汉堡、披萨、薯条（合计 81%）
- **低占比组**：炸鸡、塔可、寿司（合计 19%）

#### 尾段归纳常识特征

- 汉堡、披萨、薯条属于 **西式/本地传统快餐**。
- 寿司、塔可属于 **外来/舶来食物**。
- 结论：美国青少年更偏爱本地传统快餐。

**你的练习及修改**：

原句：
> The pie chart shows the fast food(processed food) preferences of young people in the US. In general,this report indicates that the percentage of fast food preferred is divided unevenly.
> In detial, hamburgers,pizza and french fries makes up more than 80%

修改后：
> The pie chart shows **the proportions of** fast food (**processed food**) preferred by young people in the US. In general, this report indicates that **these preferences are** divided unevenly.
>
> In detail, hamburgers, pizza and French fries **make up** more than 80% of the total.

**错误点**：
- `detial` → **detail**
- `makes up` 主语是复数 → **make up**
- `french fries` → **French fries**（专有名词大写）

#### 参考结尾

> To sum up, this graph shows that young Americans prefer local fast food, such as hamburgers, pizza and French fries, which make up more than 80% of the total.

## 四、静态多饼图：C11T1 全球用水（Multiple Pie Charts）

### 1. 题目背景

- **图表类型**：6 个饼图，展示 6 个地区在三种用途上的用水比例。
- **三种用途**：农业（Agriculture）、工业（Industry）、家庭（Domestic）。
- **六个地区**：北美、南美、欧洲、非洲、中亚、东南亚。

### 2. 解题策略：变表法

将饼图信息转化为表格，便于比较：

| 地区 | 农业 | 工业 | 家庭 |
|------|------|------|------|
| Central Asia | 88% | 5% | 7% |
| South East Asia | 81% | 7% | 12% |
| Africa | 84% | 10% | 6% |
| South America | 71% | 10% | 9% |
| North America | 39% | 48% | 13% |
| Europe | 32% | 53% | 15% |

**横轴**：用水目的（3 项，便于横向对比）。  
**纵轴**：地区（6 项，可按发达/发展中归类）。

### 3. 五段式结构

| 段落 | 句数 | 内容 |
|------|------|------|
| 首段 | 2 句 | 改写 + 总括（不均分） |
| 主体段 1 | 2 句 | 农业：占比最大且差异显著 |
| 主体段 2 | 2 句 | 工业：与农业相反 |
| 主体段 3 | 2 句 | 家庭：各地区都较小 |
| 尾段 | 1 句 | 归纳特征：发达 vs 发展中 |

### 4. 首段改写 + 总括

> The pie charts show the proportion of water consumed for three purposes in six different regions around the globe.
>
> In general, this report indicates that the percentage of water used is divided unevenly.

### 5. 主体段 1：农业（Agriculture）

**课件总括句**：

> Water consumption in agriculture accounts for the maximal percentage compared with that in other two purposes and a dramatic difference can be seen among these areas.

**更自然版本**：

> Water consumption in agriculture accounts for **the largest percentage** compared with **that** in the other two purposes, and a dramatic difference can be seen among these areas.

**课件细节句**：

> To be more specific, two thirds of them use more than 70% of water, and Central Asia consumes the highest amount (88%), doubling that of Europe, which is the lowest.

**要点**：
- `maximal` → 建议改为 **largest / highest**。
- `two thirds of them` 中 them 指代 six regions。
- `doubling that of Europe` 中 that 指代 percentage / amount。

### 6. 主体段 2：工业（Industry）

**课件例句**：

> Among them, Europe, using the least amount of water in agriculture, consumes the largest in industry, which is 53%, whereas the area that uses the maximal proportion in agriculture only uses the minimal (5%).

**更自然版本**：

> Among them, Europe, **which uses** the least amount of water in agriculture, consumes the largest proportion in industry (53%), whereas the area with the highest agricultural usage only uses the smallest proportion (5%) for industry.

**要点**：
- `using` 作分词修饰 Europe 可以，但加 which 更清晰。
- `maximal / minimal` 建议改为 **highest / smallest**。

### 7. 主体段 3：家庭（Domestic）

**课件例句**：

> In detail, the overall proportion is below 20% and the same minimal usage in water can be seen in both Central Asia and South East Asia, which is 7%.

**更自然版本**：

> In detail, the overall proportion of domestic water use is below 20%, and the same minimal usage can be seen in both Central Asia and South East Asia, at 7%.

### 8. 尾段：归纳特征

> To sum up, this graph shows that developed regions tend to use more water for industry, while developing areas use more for agriculture.

**要点**：静态图结尾不要重复比大小，要归纳 **常识性特征**。

### 9. 你的练习及修改

原句：
> In detail,the water used for agricultural makes up the maximal percentage,which account for morn than 70% in most areas.The maximal area is central Asia,which takes up 88%,being 2 times morn than that of Europe.
> Whereas,industral shows deffrent tend,

修改后：
> In detail, water used for agriculture accounts for the largest percentage, **making up** more than 70% in most areas. Central Asia has the highest proportion (88%), which is more than twice as large as that of Europe.
>
> In contrast, industry shows a different trend.

**错误点**：
- `agricultural` → **agriculture**（名词作定语）
- `maximal percentage` → **largest percentage**
- `which account for` → **making up** 或 **accounting for**（避免从句主谓不一致）
- `morn than` → **more than**
- `2 times morn than` → **more than twice as large as**
- `central Asia` → **Central Asia**（专有名词大写）
- `industral` → **industry**；`deffrent tend` → **different trend**

## 五、核心词汇与句型

### 1. 占比表达

| 表达 | 例句 |
|------|------|
| account for | Agriculture **accounts for** 80% of water use. |
| make up | Hamburgers **make up** 33% of the total. |
| take up | Industry **takes up** 53% in Europe. |
| occupy | Industrial use **occupies** nearly half. |
| constitute | These three categories **constitute** over 80%. |

**注意**：
- **make up** 主语是“构成部分”，强调“组成”。
- **take up** 更强调“占据（时间/空间/比例）”。
- 雅思饼图中 **account for / make up** 最常用。

### 2. 最值表达

| 含义 | 推荐表达 |
|------|---------|
| 最大值 | the highest / largest percentage / proportion / figure |
| 第二大 | the second highest / largest percentage |
| 最小值 | the lowest / smallest percentage / proportion |
| 最高点 | reach a peak / peak at |
| 最低点 | reach the lowest point / bottom out |
| 保持中间 | remain in the middle / rank in the middle |

**避坑**：
- ❌ maximal / minimal proportion（太数学化，不自然）
- ❌ most / least percentage（搭配错误）
- ❌ maintain the middle number/figure（中式表达）

### 3. 倍数表达

| 含义 | 推荐表达 |
|------|---------|
| A 是 B 的两倍 | A is **twice as large as** B. |
| A 是 B 的两倍多 | A is **more than twice as large as** B. |
| A 超过 B 一倍 | A is **more than double** B. |
| A 是 B 的一半 | A is **half the size of** B. |

**避坑**：
- ❌ `2 times more than`（有歧义：是 200% 还是 300%？）
- ❌ `being 2 times more than that of the least`
- ✅ `more than twice as large as the smallest figure`

### 4. 指代词

| 指代对象 | 表达 |
|---------|------|
| 前者 / 后者 | the former / the latter |
| 单数名词 | that |
| 复数名词 | those |
| 图表/报告 | this graph / this report |
| 数据 | this figure / these figures |

**例句**：

> The CO₂ emissions in the UK were higher than **those** in Sweden.
>
> The proportion in Italy was much larger than **that** in Portugal.

### 5. 衔接词

| 类别 | 词汇 |
|------|------|
| 顺序 | Overall / In general / To be more specific / In detail / Afterwards / To sum up |
| 对比 | while / whereas / however / in contrast / on the other hand |
| 并列/补充 | meanwhile / at the same time / in addition |
| 因果 | because / since / so / therefore（静态图慎用强因果解释数据） |

## 六、常见错误总结

### 1. 时态错误

- 动态图用 **一般过去时**。
- 静态图无具体时间用 **一般现在时**。

### 2. 冠词错误

- ❌ in UK / in USA → ✅ in **the** UK / in **the** USA
- ❌ top of chart → ✅ **the** top of **the** chart

### 3. 介词错误

- ❌ at 1977 → ✅ in 1977
- ❌ at the end（太模糊）→ ✅ by the end of the period

### 4. 词汇搭配错误

| 错误 | 正确 |
|------|------|
| most percentage | the highest percentage |
| least proportion | the smallest proportion |
| maximal percentage | the largest percentage |
| minimal proportion | the smallest proportion |
| finish point | final point / end point |

### 5. 标点空格

- 逗号、句号后必须加空格。
- 括号前建议加空格：`peak (11mt)`。

## 八、本课核心思想

1. **动态图**：用四段式结构保证逻辑清晰，用指代词和分词结构提升句式复杂度。
2. **静态单饼图**：首段点明均分/不均分，主体按大小分组，结尾归纳常识特征。
3. **静态多饼图**：用“变表法”梳理数据，横向+纵向交叉对比，五段式结构。
4. **通用原则**：先写完，再写好；动词优先；不为高级词牺牲准确性。
