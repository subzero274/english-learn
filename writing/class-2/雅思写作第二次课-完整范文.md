## 范文 1：动态曲线图（C11T3 CO₂ 排放）

> **题目**：The graph below shows average carbon dioxide (CO₂) emissions per person in the United Kingdom, Sweden, Italy and Portugal between 1967 and 2007.

The line graph shows average emissions of CO₂ per person in metric tonnes in four countries during a long period of 40 years since 1967. In general, this report indicates that there were different trends in average carbon dioxide emissions.

The CO₂ emissions in the UK started from the highest point (11mt) and dropped slowly to the lowest point (9mt) in this group. The UK always emitted the most CO₂ among the four countries over the period. Meanwhile, that in the latter country, Sweden, rose quickly to about 10.5mt in 1977, which was the peak. It then fell significantly to the final point in 2007 (almost 5.2mt). This figure was much lower than its starting point in 1967, which was around 8.2mt.

In contrast, Italy and Portugal both experienced an upward trend. Between then, there was a dramatic increase in that of Italy, which was from about 4.5mt to 7.8mt, surpassing that of Sweden in 1987. Although the trend of Portugal was the same as that of Italy, its emissions were the lowest, and its final point was equal to that of Sweden.

To sum up, this graph shows that the UK always emitted the most CO₂ among these countries over these years.

---

### 范文 1 解析

| 段落 | 功能 | 技巧 |
|------|------|------|
| 首段 | 改写 + 总括 | 包含时间、地点、对象、单位；总括用 different trends |
| 主体段 1 | 下降组：UK + Sweden | UK 始终最高；Sweden 先升后降，终点低于起点 |
| 主体段 2 | 上升组：Italy + Portugal | Italy 超越 Sweden；Portugal 始终最低但终点与 Sweden 持平 |
| 尾段 | 比大小 | the UK always emitted the most |

**亮点句型**：
- `that in the latter country` 指代 Sweden 的排放
- `surpassing that of Sweden in 1987` 分词表结果
- `its final point was equal to that of Sweden` that 指代 final point

## 范文 2：静态单饼图（美国青少年快餐偏好）

> **题目**：The pie chart shows the proportion of fast food preferred by teenagers in the USA.

The pie chart illustrates the uneven distribution of fast food preferences among teenagers in the USA. Overall, hamburgers, pizza and French fries are clearly the most popular choices, while the remaining options are much less preferred.

In detail, hamburgers make up the largest proportion, at 33%, followed by pizza at 26% and French fries at 22%. These three categories account for more than 80% of the total. In contrast, fried chicken and tacos only represent 9% and 7% respectively. Sushi is the least popular, at just 3%.

To sum up, this chart shows that young Americans tend to prefer local Western-style fast food, such as hamburgers, pizza and French fries, rather than imported options like sushi or tacos.

---

### 范文 2 解析

| 段落 | 功能 | 技巧 |
|------|------|------|
| 首段 | 改写 + 总括 | 点明 uneven distribution |
| 主体段 | 按大小分组 | 高占比组（81%）+ 低占比组（19%） |
| 尾段 | 归纳常识特征 | 本地西式快餐 vs 外来食物 |

**亮点表达**：
- `make up the largest proportion, at 33%`
- `account for more than 80% of the total`
- `tend to prefer local Western-style fast food`

## 范文 3：静态多饼图（C11T1 全球用水）

> **题目**：The pie charts show the proportion of water consumed for three purposes in six different regions around the globe.

The pie charts show the proportion of water consumed for three purposes in six different regions around the globe. In general, this report indicates that the percentage of water used is divided unevenly.

Water consumption in agriculture accounts for the largest percentage compared with that in the other two purposes, and a dramatic difference can be seen among these areas. To be more specific, two thirds of the regions use more than 70% of water for agriculture, and Central Asia consumes the highest amount (88%), which is more than twice as large as that of Europe, the lowest.

In contrast, industry shows a different trend. Among them, Europe, which uses the least amount of water in agriculture, consumes the largest proportion in industry (53%), whereas the area with the highest agricultural usage only uses the smallest proportion (5%) for industry.

Domestic water use, however, accounts for a relatively small share in all regions, with the overall proportion below 20%. The same minimal usage can be seen in both Central Asia and South East Asia, at 7%.

To sum up, this graph shows that developed regions tend to use more water for industry, while developing areas use more for agriculture.

---

### 范文 3 解析

| 段落 | 功能 | 技巧 |
|------|------|------|
| 首段 | 改写 + 总括 | 点明 divided unevenly |
| 主体段 1 | 农业 | 占比最大 + 地区差异大（中亚 88% vs 欧洲约 32%） |
| 主体段 2 | 工业 | 与农业相反（欧洲工业 53%，中亚工业 5%） |
| 主体段 3 | 家庭 | 各地区都较小，最小值 7% |
| 尾段 | 归纳特征 | 发达地区工业用水多，发展中地区农业用水多 |

**亮点句型**：
- `that in the other two purposes` 指代 percentage
- `more than twice as large as that of Europe`
- `the area with the highest agricultural usage` 介词短语作定语
- `developed regions..., while developing areas...` 对比从句

## 写作时可套用的万能句型

### 动态图

1. **首段改写**：
   > The line graph shows ... in [数量] countries during a long period of [时间段] since [起始年份].

2. **总括**：
   > In general, this report indicates that there were different trends in ....

3. **描述峰值**：
   > ... rose quickly to about [数字] in [年份], which was the peak.

4. **描述超越**：
   > ... surpassed that of [国家] in [年份].

5. **尾段比大小**：
   > To sum up, this graph shows that [国家] always emitted the most ... among these countries over these years.

### 静态图

1. **首段总括不均分**：
   > The pie chart illustrates the uneven distribution of ...

2. **占比最大**：
   > ... accounts for the largest percentage, at [数字].

3. **倍数对比**：
   > ... which is more than twice as large as that of ....

4. **相反情况**：
   > In contrast, ... shows a different trend.

5. **尾段归纳特征**：
   > To sum up, this graph shows that developed regions tend to ..., while developing areas ....
