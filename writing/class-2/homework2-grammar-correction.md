## 二、语法修正版

The bar charts show **what** graduate and postgraduate students in **the** UK who did not go into full-time work did after leaving college in 2008. Overall, further study was the most popular choice for both groups, while voluntary work was the least common.

In detail, graduate students **preferred** further study, **with 29,665 students choosing this option**, which was the **highest** figure in the chart. Part-time work and unemployment had similar figures, **at 17,735 and 16,235 respectively**. Voluntary work was the smallest category, with only 3,500 students.

A slightly different pattern can be seen among postgraduates. The number of students choosing part-time work was noticeably high, reaching a similar level to those pursuing further study, **at 2,535 and 2,725 respectively**. Similar to graduate students, voluntary work was the least popular choice, with only 345 students, while unemployment stood at 1,625.

## 五、主要问题总结

### 1. 概述段（Overview）缺失
原开头段第二句 "The figure were different in graduate and postgraduate students" 太笼统，没有概括主要趋势。

**建议改为：**
> Overall, further study was the most popular option for both graduates and postgraduates, while voluntary work was chosen by the smallest number of students in each group.

### 2. 静态图误用动态动词
这是严重错误。本题是 2008 年一年的静态数据，不能用 rose / dropped / increased。

❌ The percentage of students choosing part-time work **rose** noticeably.

✅ The proportion of students choosing part-time work **was** noticeably high.

### 3. "separately" 误用
描述两个对应数据时，应使用 **respectively**。

❌ Part-time work and Unemployment had samily data, separately 17735 and 16235.

✅ Part-time work and unemployment had similar figures, at 17,735 and 16,235 respectively.

### 4. 词汇重复
全文反复使用 number / charts / students。应替换为：
- number → figure / proportion / category / amount
- charts → chart / bar chart
- students → graduates / postgraduates / those / this group

### 5. 数据格式
英文中千位分隔符用逗号：
- 29665 → 29,665
- 17735 → 17,735

### 6. 结尾段多余
雅思 Task 1 不需要 "To sum up" 结尾段，总趋势应放在 overview 段。

## 六、6.0 目标版本参考

The bar charts compare the numbers of UK graduates and postgraduates who did not enter full-time employment in 2008, according to what they did after finishing college.

Overall, further study was the most popular option for both groups, while voluntary work attracted the smallest number of students. Additionally, the figures for graduate students were considerably higher than those for postgraduates in all categories.

In detail, among graduate students, further study was chosen by the highest number of students, at 29,665. Part-time work and unemployment had similar figures, at 17,735 and 16,235 respectively, while only 3,500 graduates did voluntary work.

A similar pattern can be seen among postgraduates, though the numbers were much lower. Further study remained the most common choice, with 2,725 students, closely followed by part-time work at 2,535. Unemployment accounted for 1,625, and voluntary work was again the least popular option, at just 345.
