The time I got up early was last month, on July 1st, which was a Saturday. I decided to go on a weekend trip with my wife
and a friend of mine. We planned to visit Wulanchabu City, which is about a two-hour train ride from Beijing. The main
reason we had to get up so early was that our train left at 8 o’clock in the morning, and we still needed to get to the
station, buy breakfast, and go through security. So I set my alarm for 6 a.m., which is quite unusual for me because I
normally sleep in on weekends.
After arriving at the railway station, I grabbed a coffee to wake myself up. Then I caught the train, and luckily, since
the 2026 World Cup was on, I watched the Argentina vs Cape Verde match on my phone. The exciting game really boosted my
energy even though I felt exhausted at first. When we finally arrived at Wulanchabu railway station, we rented a car and
began our trip.
