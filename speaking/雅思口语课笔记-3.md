## 一、Part 2 事件类话题框架（以"环球影城"为例）

**万能三段结构**（课件核心）：

| 段落 | 内容 | 功能 |
|---|---|---|
| Intro | 是什么 + 时间/地点/人物 | 交代背景 |
| Main points | 体验 + 难易程度 + 喜爱原因 | 展开细节 |
| Conclusion | 评价 | 收尾升华 |

**高分句型与表达**：
- 开头：*So, this actually happened last winter holiday... I'd been looking forward to it for ages / counting down the days.*
- 比喻句型：**Doing something was like...**（……就像……一样）
  - *Seeing that giant T-Rex up close was like stepping right into the movie.*
  - *Opening the exam paper and seeing a question I'd prepared for was like winning the lottery.*
  - *Getting back home after a long tiring day and just lying on the sofa was like being in a different world.*
- 体验词：buzzing with energy、having a blast、stepping right into the movies、a dream come true、made my day、tested my patience、almost backed out
- 难易程度：*I wouldn't say it was difficult in terms of skills — the only challenging thing was the waiting. We stood in line for about an hour and a half, and that really tested my patience.*
- 评价收尾：*It was absolutely unforgettable. Honestly, I've been dying to go back ever since, and I'm already planning my next visit.*

**可迁移素材库**（课件列的两大话题池）：
- 户外/运动/冒险/探索类：go bungee jumping、go skydiving、go rock climbing、go hiking in the mountains、go camping in the wild、go surfing、go scuba diving、visit a new country、explore a new city、take a spontaneous trip、try local food for the first time
- 创意/挑战/技能/娱乐类：try a new hobby、learn to play an instrument、perform on stage、join a competition、go to a concert、go to a music festival、go to a live sports match、go to a theme park、ride a roller coaster、go to a karaoke night、go dancing at a club

→ 同一个"环球影城"故事可套用到 an exciting activity / a place you visited / an unforgettable day 等多个题卡。

## 二、Part 1：最受欢迎的运动（Popular Sports）

**答题逻辑（R&E：Reason & Explanation）**——不追求标准答案，重在解释"为什么受欢迎"：

1. **Easy to learn** 门槛低：*Anyone can do them, from kids to grandparents.*
2. **Really accessible** 设施方便：*You can do them anywhere* —— 公园、小区楼下、学校操场（对比滑雪要去特定雪场）。
3. **Low cost** 成本低：*You don't need expensive equipment*（对比滑雪昂贵的门票和装备）。
4. **Time-friendly** 省时间：*A quick game takes about half an hour — that's great for people with busy schedules*（对比滑雪通常耗一整天）。

总结句：*Basically, they are popular because they are cheap, quick, and simple.*

**示例回答结构**：
- 观点：*Badminton and running are probably the most popular sports in China.*
- 理由1（设施/成本）：*They're really accessible — you don't need expensive equipment, and you can do them anywhere, like in parks or neighborhoods.*
- 理由2（时间）：*They're time-friendly — a quick game takes about half an hour, which is great for people with busy schedules.*
- 理由3（门槛）：*They're easy to learn — anyone can do them, from kids to grandparents.*

## 三、Part 3：为什么人们热衷危险活动（Dangerous Activities）

三大心理动机：

1. **追求刺激与活在当下（Thrill & Adrenaline）**
   - adrenaline rush（肾上腺素飙升）
   - *It makes you feel really alive and in the moment* —— 极度专注带来的"心流"体验：*All you can think about is that experience.*
2. **个人挑战与成就感（Personal Challenge & Achievement）**
   - 战胜恐惧：*a sense of achievement*
   - *You feel like a superhero.*
3. **逃离日常与释放压力（Escape from Routine）**
   - *It's a total escape from boring everyday stuff.*

## 五、听写重点词汇

- 形容词：intense（激烈的/刺激的）、magical（神奇的）、unforgettable（难忘的）、packed（挤爆的/拥挤的）
- 搭配：scream on the rides（在过山车上尖叫）、take goofy photos（拍搞怪照片）、get the best angle/view（获得最佳视角）、have a blast / enjoy oneself（玩得开心）、made my day（让我一天都很开心）

## 六、校对说明（本次修正的识别/语法问题）

1. **"Rarely accessible" → "Really accessible"**：语音转写错误。课件原文为 really accessible，且语义上"几乎不可用"与"受欢迎"自相矛盾。口语中 rarely / really 发音易混，需特别注意。
2. **"kick off my day" → "made my day"**：kick off 意为"开始（活动）"，与环球影城语境不符；课件原文为 *it genuinely made my day*（让我一天都开心）。
3. **"stood in line for like an hour and a half"**：like 作口语填充词可用，但更规范的说法是 *for about an hour and a half*。
