## 四、运动受欢迎的原因（Part 1）

| 表达 | 含义 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| easy to learn | 容易学 | Anyone can do them, from kids to grandparents. |
| really accessible | 很方便/容易获得 | They're really accessible — you can do them anywhere. |

| busy schedules | 忙碌的日程 | ...which is great for people with busy schedules. |
| expensive equipment | 昂贵的装备 | — |

> ⚠️ 易混：rarely accessible（几乎不可用）≠ really accessible（很方便）。

## 八、本次校正的易错点

| 错误表达 | 正确表达 | 说明 |
|---|---|---|
| rarely accessible | **really accessible** | 发音相近，但意思相反 |
| kick off my day | **made my day** | kick off 表示"开始"，不符合语境 |
| for like an hour and a half | **for about an hour and a half** | like 作填充词较口语化，about 更规范 |

## 九、万能高分句型模板

1. **开头背景**：
   > So, this actually happened last winter holiday... I'd been looking forward to it for ages.

2. **比喻句型**：
   > Doing something was like + doing something else.
   > - Seeing that giant T-Rex up close was like stepping right into the movie.
   > - Opening the exam paper and seeing a question I'd prepared for was like winning the lottery.

3. **总结受欢迎原因**：
   > Basically, they are popular because they are cheap, quick, and simple.

4. **收尾评价**：
   > It was absolutely unforgettable. Honestly, I've been dying to go back ever since.
